# Rolograph

## Product

Rolograph turns events into actionable relationship maps.

Instead of storing a flat attendee list, Rolograph connects people, companies, conversations, introductions, opportunities, and follow-up actions in an interactive graph.

## Problem

At networking events, attendees meet many potentially useful people but quickly lose the context around each interaction.

Traditional contact apps store names and companies, but they do not show:

- Why someone matters
- How people are connected
- What was discussed
- Who introduced whom
- What follow-up action should happen next

## Solution

Rolograph creates an event-specific relationship graph.

Users can explore people and organizations before an event, capture interactions during the event, and generate prioritized follow-ups afterward.

## Target User

Builders, founders, engineers, recruiters, investors, and community organizers attending professional events.

## MVP

The MVP will support:

1. An event page
2. Person nodes
3. Company nodes
4. Relationship edges
5. A visual graph
6. Person detail views
7. Interaction notes
8. Follow-up actions
9. Basic relationship priority scoring

## Core User Flow

1. Open an event
2. View the relationship graph
3. Select a person
4. See why they are relevant
5. Record an interaction
6. Add notes and a next action
7. Update the graph
8. Review prioritized follow-ups

## Out of Scope

The hackathon version will not include:

- LinkedIn scraping
- Automatic identity resolution
- CRM integrations
- Multi-user collaboration
- A general-purpose social network
- Fully autonomous relationship management

## Success Criteria

By the final submission, users must be able to:

- Open the deployed application
- Explore the Cursor Miami relationship graph
- Inspect people and companies
- Record an interaction
- Add a follow-up action
- See the relationship graph update
